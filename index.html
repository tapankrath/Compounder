Management Rules & Playbook

Checked in this order:

1. Time exit. Inside the time-exit window, close regardless. Gamma and assignment risk outweigh the little premium left.

2. Stop loss. Close when the loss reaches the set multiple of the credit collected.

3. Take profit. Close at the profit target. With a week or more left, redeploy a fresh spread from the new price.

4. Roll. When a short strike's delta reaches the roll level (or price breaks it), roll down/up and out for a net credit. If no credit is available, close instead.

5. Adjust. When the short put's delta reaches the adjust level, sell a call spread to form an iron condor. If it's already a condor, move the untested side closer to collect more credit.

6. Press the winner. If price runs away from the put spread early (short Δ has fallen to ~60% of the target or less, 25%+ of max profit, a week or more left, profit target not yet hit), roll the put spread up to the target delta for extra credit.

Prices and deltas are Black-Scholes estimates from the IV you enter. Real chains have skew (puts usually price richer), so check fills and the cost to close against your broker.
